Research

Learning and inference with shifted or incomplete data.

I develop statistical methods for transfer learning and classification when populations differ or labels are incomplete. My work uses semiparametric inference, mixture models, and density ratios. I also study prediction intervals and reliability.

Explore my work on Google Scholar →
01

Transfer learning

Adapting prediction and inference across populations with label, group-label, open-set, and continuous target shift.

02

Positive-unlabeled learning

Estimating and classifying with labeled positives and unlabeled examples, from density-ratio models to in-context learning.

03

Semiparametric inference

Using mixture models, density ratios, and empirical likelihood for estimation and uncertainty quantification.

04

Classification and diagnostics

Studying classification under noisy labels and ROC analysis when diagnostic reference standards are imperfect.

05

Missing data

Developing estimation and model-checking tools when missingness depends on outcomes that are not observed.

06

Prediction and reliability

Constructing prediction intervals for future events and failures, including from heterogeneous reliability data.
